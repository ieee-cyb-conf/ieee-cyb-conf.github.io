Call for Special Sessions

The IEEE International Conference on Cybernetics 2027 (IEEE CYB Conf 2027) invites proposals for Special Sessions to be held during the conference in Rende (Cosenza), Italy, May 10–13, 2027. Special Sessions provide a focused forum on innovative, emerging, and cross-disciplinary topics complementing the regular technical program and exploring frontier areas of Cybernetics and the conference theme "{{ site.data.cyb_data.theme }}".

Special Sessions

Aims and Scope

Special Sessions aim to bring together international researchers, industry practitioners, and domain experts to present and discuss breakthroughs in specific, highly topical domains. Proposals covering novel theoretical models, cutting-edge applications, experimental benchmarks, and multidisciplinary paradigms within the scope of Cybernetics and humanoid robotics are warmly encouraged.

Emerging & Niche Topics

Focus on specialized or cutting-edge cybernetics domains that may not be fully represented in the regular tracks.

Community Building

Foster vibrant collaborations across academic institutions, research labs, and industrial partners worldwide.

Guidelines

Proposal Content & Format

Special Session proposals should be prepared as a concise document (PDF format, 1–3 pages) and must include the following information:

  • Title and Acronym: Clear, concise title and acronym for the proposed Special Session.
  • Aims and Scientific Rationale: A 200–400 word description outlining the scientific background, importance, timeliness, and relevance to IEEE CYB Conf 2027.
  • List of Topics: Specific topics and sub-areas covered by the Special Session.
  • Organizers' Information: Names, affiliations, email addresses, and short bios (100–150 words) highlighting relevant research and organizational experience.
  • Tentative List of Papers / Authors: A preliminary list of prospective authors and working paper titles (at least 4–6 submissions expected).
Process

Session Organization & Paper Review

1
Proposal Review: Proposals are evaluated by the Workshop & Special Sessions Chairs and General Chairs based on scientific quality, novelty, and relevance.
2
Paper Submission & Peer Review: Papers submitted to accepted Special Sessions undergo the exact same peer-review process as regular conference papers, adhering to IEEE standards. All manuscripts must strictly adhere to the official IEEE Conference Templates.
3
Minimum Paper Requirement: A Special Session requires a minimum of 4 accepted and registered papers to be scheduled as an independent oral session. If fewer papers are accepted, they will be incorporated into regular conference sessions.
4
Session Chairs: Organizers of accepted sessions will be appointed as Session Chairs during the conference event.
