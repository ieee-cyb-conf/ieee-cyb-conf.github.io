Technical Program Committee

The TPC members will be well-known researchers and experts from Academia and Industry, coming from all areas of the world (America, Europe, Africa, Middle-East, Asia, Australia, etc.) and actively working on the topics of the CYB Conf conference.

We aim to have at least 100 members to effectively and efficiently support the review phase. The TPC will be formed later and will be mainly based on the TCs of the Cybernetics area of IEEE SMCS and the Editorial Board of IEEE Transactions on Cybernetics.
