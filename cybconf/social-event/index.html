Social Event & Dinner

Discover the open-air MAB museum and enjoy the social dinner at the Norman-Swabian Castle of Cosenza.

Outdoor Museum MAB

The Museo all'Aperto Bilotti (MAB) is an open-air museum along Corso Mazzini, the main street of Cosenza, featuring sculptures by world-famous artists such as Salvador Dalí, Giorgio de Chirico and Mimmo Rotella.

Learn more about MAB

Social Dinner at Cosenza Castle

The social dinner will take place at the Norman-Swabian Castle of Cosenza, dominating the city from the top of Pancrazio hill, a magnificent setting to enjoy Calabrian cuisine and networking with colleagues from all over the world.
