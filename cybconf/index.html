Join us at IEEE CYB Conf 2027

We are delighted to invite you to the IEEE International Conference on Cybernetics 2027 (IEEE CYB Conf 2027), which will be held at the Conference Center of the University of Calabria (UNICAL) in Rende (Cosenza), Calabria, Italy, from {{site.data.cyb_data.dates.conferenceDates}}. The conference brings together researchers and practitioners from Academia and Industry, coming from all areas of the world, actively working on the topics of Cybernetics.

The theme of this edition is "{{ site.data.cyb_data.theme }}". The program will include keynote speeches, workshops, tutorials, special sessions, an industry/research panel, poster and demo sessions, a PhD Forum and a Women in Cybernetics networking meeting — all set in Calabria, the southern-most tip of Italy and the land of Pythagoras, with over 800 km of coastline, green mountains and a wonderful Mediterranean cuisine.

Sincerely,
The IEEE CYB Conf 2027 Organizing Committee

Venue & Location

The University of Calabria

IEEE CYB Conf 2027 will take place at the Conference Center of the University of Calabria (UNICAL) in Rende (Cosenza), Italy. Established in 1972, UNICAL is a large university with almost 25,000 students and, according to CENSIS, it is ranked n.1 among the large Italian universities.

The venue is just 50 minutes from Lamezia Terme International Airport and 15 minutes by train from the Tyrrhenian coast and the beautiful town of Paola.
